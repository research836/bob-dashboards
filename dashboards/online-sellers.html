Two ways puppies are sold online

Online marketplace

A website where breeders list their own puppies. The breeder handles the sale directly with the buyer, including payment and pickup or shipping.

In this analysis: Puppies.com, GoodDog, AKC Marketplace, Greenfield Puppies, Lancaster Puppies

Online broker

A third-party company that handles the sale for the breeder. The buyer pays the broker, and the broker arranges delivery, so buyers usually never deal with the breeder.

In this analysis: PuppySpot, Pawrade

The big picture

BOB tracks 25 puppy websites. These seven are by far the largest, each with at least 250 audited sellers.

These numbers are not a count of everyone selling puppies online. We estimate there are more than 100,000 online sellers, and we are auditing them now. This page includes only the sellers we have fully audited and can report on, so it shows patterns among those sellers. The numbers will grow as our audit continues.

Warning signs on GoodDog profiles

“Warning signs” are based on what sellers post on their own profiles. In general, breeders with more than four breeding females who sell puppies sight unseen need a USDA license, and many states license breeders above a set number of breeding females. Some of these sellers may already hold a license we have not yet matched, so these are not counts of unlicensed breeders.

How we counted

Which sites

BOB tracks 25 puppy websites. This page covers the seven with at least 250 documented sellers. The next largest site has about 50.

Who counts as a seller

A breeder or broker BOB documented selling on the site in any year from 2022 to 2026, counted once per site. We estimate there are more than 100,000 online sellers and are auditing them now. This page includes only sellers we have fully audited, so the numbers show patterns among audited sellers, not the total number of people selling online.

License status

Each seller’s current status in BOB’s database: active USDA license, state license only, a USDA or state license that has ended, or no license found. A few records with unclear status are grouped as “Other.”

Why there are no growth trends

BOB began documenting some sites, such as AKC Marketplace and Greenfield Puppies, in 2025. Year-to-year changes would mostly reflect when research started, so this page reports totals instead.

Audit in progress

Profiles under review come from BOB’s case-building database (Puppies.com and AKC Marketplace) and a state-by-state review of GoodDog breeder profiles. They have not been verified or matched to license records yet, and one seller can appear on more than one site, so they count profiles, not people.
