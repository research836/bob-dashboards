Leaving the USDA system

A USDA license ends when the breeder or broker cancels it, or when the USDA suspends or revokes it. Losing a federal license does not always mean leaving the business.

This is preliminary data. As we continue auditing online marketplaces, we are finding more cancelled licensees still selling, so we expect these numbers to grow.

Who is watching

Federal and state licenses overlap. Some breeders answer to both agencies, while others answer only to their state, including hundreds who once held a USDA license.
