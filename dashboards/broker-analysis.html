Who are the puppy brokers?

A look at the brokers in Bailing Out Benji’s research: the middlemen who buy puppies from breeders and resell them to pet stores and online buyers.

What is a puppy broker?

A broker is a third party that handles the sale for the breeder. Brokers buy puppies from breeders, often several at a time, and resell them to pet stores or directly to buyers online. When a broker is involved, it can be harder for buyers to trace a puppy back to the breeder who raised it.

What USDA inspections show

Brokers often keep few animals on site, so they may be inspected less often than breeders. Only USDA inspection findings are counted.

How we counted

Which brokers

Brokers in BOB’s research database who are currently active: those with an active USDA or state license, those with no license found who are documented selling, and former licensees documented selling to pet stores or online in the last three years. Brokers who also or only sell cats are included. Records still under review are left out.

Pet store connections

Sales to pet stores documented in 2024, 2025, or 2026, mostly from health certificates (CVIs) and public records. A store counts once per broker no matter how many shipments it received.

USDA violations

A broker counts as cited if a USDA inspection in the past five years found at least one violation. Serious means at least one direct or critical violation.
