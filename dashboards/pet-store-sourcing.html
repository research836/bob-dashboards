Where do pet store puppies come from?

A look at the breeders and brokers supplying the open pet stores Bailing Out Benji tracks: how they are licensed, where they are based, and what their USDA inspection records show.

Supplier connections documented 2024 to 2026 · Data as of November 2, 2026 · Shown as totals only

The big picture

These numbers reflect the supplier connections we have documented so far, mostly from health certificates and public records. Most stores buy from more suppliers than we can identify, so the true numbers are likely higher.

Who supplies pet stores

License status of the breeders and brokers documented selling to these stores.

“No license found” means BOB found no USDA or state license on record. Breeders with four or fewer breeding females are exempt from USDA licensing, so this group can include small breeders who are not required to hold a license.

How stores buy their puppies

Most stores buy from far away, and many buy through brokers, companies that collect puppies from breeders and resell them. When a broker is involved, it can be harder for buyers to trace a puppy back to the breeder.

How we counted

Which stores

Pet stores BOB tracks that are currently open, based on our most recent status check. Stores that have closed or no longer sell puppies are left out.

Which suppliers

Breeders and brokers documented selling to a store in 2024, 2025, or 2026, counted once per store. Connections come mostly from health certificates (CVIs) and public records, so they are a floor, not a full list.

USDA violations

A supplier counts as cited if a USDA inspection in the past five years found at least one violation. Serious means at least one direct or critical violation, the most severe categories. Suppliers without USDA inspections on file are not counted as cited.

License status

Each supplier’s current status in BOB’s database. A supplier whose USDA license has since ended may have still held it when it sold to the store.

Totals only

This page reports totals and does not identify individual stores, breeders, or brokers. Store-level profiles are available in BOB’s pet store directory.
