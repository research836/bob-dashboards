How they sell their puppies

Breeders reach buyers in different ways depending on how they are licensed. Federally licensed breeders mostly supply pet stores, while state-licensed and unlicensed breeders mostly sell directly to families online.

How we counted

Which breeders

Breeders in BOB’s research database who are currently active: those with an active USDA or state license, those with no license found who are documented selling, and former licensees documented selling to pet stores or online in the last three years. Breeders who also or only breed cats are included. Records still under review are left out.

Size

Adult dog counts come from inspection reports and state records. Only licensed breeders usually have one. A few counts over 1,000 that appear to be data entry errors are left out, and we report the typical (median) breeder rather than an average.

Sales channels

Documented sales from 2022 to 2026 to pet stores, through puppy websites (marketplaces and online brokers), on a breeder’s own website, or to brokers. A breeder can use several.

USDA violations

A breeder counts as cited if a USDA inspection in the past five years found at least one violation. Serious means at least one direct or critical violation. State inspections are not included.
